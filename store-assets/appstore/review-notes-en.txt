Kimito Link Surechigai Tsushin records where you have been with accurate location check-ins and lets you meet people who passed the same places.

SIGN-IN
Sign-in is not required to browse the first screens. To review the signed-in experience, tap the start button, check "利用規約（EULA）に同意します" (I agree to the Terms of Use / EULA), then choose "Sign in with Apple" (your own Apple ID works) or X. There is no username/password login.

GUIDELINE 5.1.2(i) (location shown on a map) - changes in this build
- Age rating: set to 18+ (Override to a Higher Age Rating).
- Privacy policy: https://surechigai.kimito.link/privacy (App Privacy URL field and in-app).
- Permission with an option to decline: before a user's location can ever appear on a map, the app asks "あなたの位置を地図に出しますか？" (Show your location on the map?) with "許可しない" (Don't Allow) and "許可して、いま地図に出す" (Allow and show now). Before the first check-in it also asks whether check-in places may be shown on other users' maps ("許可しない" / "許可する"). Declining keeps everything private.
- Manual check-in only: the app never shows a user's location on a map automatically. There is no automatic check-in and no background location. A user's position appears on other users' maps only right after the user taps "いまの場所を地図に出す" (Show my current place on the map, My Page tab) or the check-in button. It disappears after 5 minutes unless the user checks in again.
- Blocking: tap any other user's pin on the home map, then "ブロックする" (Block). Users can also block from a received envelope ("ブロック") or the report screen. Blocked users never see each other on maps, encounters or events. The block list and unblock are in My Page > "ブロックリスト".

GUIDELINE 1.2 (user-generated content)
- Users must agree to the Terms of Use (EULA) before signing in. The sign-in buttons stay disabled until the checkbox is checked.
- The terms state that there is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Violating content is removed and the user's account is suspended.
- Report: open a received envelope and tap "通報" (Report), then choose a reason. We review reports within 24 hours. Accounts reported by three different users are suspended automatically.

GUIDELINE 5.1.1(iv) (location permission)
The explanation screen shown before the system location prompt has a single "次へ" (Next) button. The user always proceeds to the iOS permission dialog and decides there.

GUIDELINE 2.1(b) / 3.1.2(c) (In-App Purchase)
The iOS app no longer contains any premium plan, subscription or In-App Purchase, and no screen refers to one. The app is free to use.

GUIDELINE 4 (iPad)
The app is iPhone-only (not built for iPad) and runs in iPhone compatibility mode on iPad. We fixed the cropped text and checked every screen in that mode.

ACCOUNT DELETION (5.1.1(v))
Users can delete their account in My Page or at https://surechigai.kimito.link/deletion.
